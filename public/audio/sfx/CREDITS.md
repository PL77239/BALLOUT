# Recorded court Foley (CC0)

These are trimmed, mono 22.05 kHz WAV derivatives of publicly available previews of the following CC0 recordings. No sampled NBA/EA game audio is included. Normalized peaks, short de-click fades; original authors retain credit below.

- bounce-1/2/3: kyles, “basketball ext dribble bounce hard surface.flac” — https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/453757/ — CC0.
- rim-1/2: reedhos, “basketball.wav” (basketball hitting a rim) — https://freesound.org/people/reedhos/sounds/244348/ — CC0.
- net-1/2: Hann90, “Basketball Net Swish Sound.wav” — https://freesound.org/people/Hann90/sounds/518048/ — CC0.
- squeak-1/2: ThibaudVaerman / BigSoundBank, “Gnashing basketball” (shoe friction) — https://bigsoundbank.com/gnashing-basketball-s0938.html — CC0.

CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
`edits.json` records source category, start time and duration in seconds. Playback uses subtle pitch variation. Backboard and slam accents reuse softened rim recordings; no separate backboard recording is claimed.
